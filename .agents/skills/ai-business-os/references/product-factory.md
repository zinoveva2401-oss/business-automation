# Integrated Product Factory v2.0.11 candidate | r9-derived

## North Star
Create products that solve a meaningful paid problem and feel like premium finished intellectual assets, not AI-generated block collections. The default completion state is a complete `RELEASE CANDIDATE — INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED`, not a draft, outline, prototype or list of next steps. Светлана is asked to review only when a genuine owner gate remains; routine product/editorial/visual/technical acceptance is closed by the professional contours and independent verifier.

## Command contract: «сделай готовый продукт на тему X»

Treat this as a parent run, not immediate writing. Route:
`opportunity/owner intent → prove problem → domain landscape → market/substitutes → founder/claims/IP check → productization decision → cheap experiment if uncertainty is material → product thesis → format/learning fit → commercial/economic fit → frozen full matrix → PRODUCT CONTENT BLUEPRINT → executor packets → whole-artifact production → multidisciplinary QA → independent verification → commercial packaging/delivery → distribution/growth → E2E purchase test → launch/money → learning → portfolio decision`.

Do not ask Svetlana to choose internal frameworks, chapter structures, spreadsheet layouts, visual systems or production techniques when professional evidence can resolve them. Do not produce a product if another route (content/tool/service/experiment/stop) is materially better.

### P10.75 — prototype before full production (blocking)

Before mass production of a meaningful product, make a representative prototype using real product content and the proposed format. Examples: several substantive book pages; working spreadsheet sheets/formulas; a functioning calculator; actual app screens and one tested user flow; one real lesson/module; or an integrated product fragment. Render, open, use or test that prototype in its intended medium and record objective evidence. A plan, wireframe, lorem ipsum, mock content or self-authored PASS is not a prototype.

Show the owner the real prototype and ask for approval of material product promise, method, format, UX and visual direction. Record approved decisions and frozen constraints in the canonical task/product source before full production. If the owner rejects or redirects it, revise the prototype and obtain the decision before scaling production. Only routine implementation choices inside the approved direction remain delegated. This gate supersedes any earlier r9 wording that allowed a producer to bypass owner review for an internal prototype.

Product Factory is format-neutral: book, workbook, spreadsheet/calculator, web product, application/software, course, toolkit/library, a justified combination, or another evidence-supported format. Do not anchor quality to a temporary launch price. A launch price around 1,990–2,000 RUB is not a quality ceiling; durable value and future price potential must be assessed from evidence without promising a price the market has not validated.

## Product subagent / council contract

The Product Factory does not rely on one “productologist” voice. For material products it runs independent passes, merged only after disagreement is visible:
`Product Strategist → Domain Methodologist → Market/Substitute Research → Customer Voice → Skeptical Buyer/Free-AI Challenger → Commercial/CRO → CFO → Legal/IP → Product UX/Artifact Architecture → Narrative/Long-Form → Semantic-Preservation Auditor → Value-Density/AHA Reviewer → Art/Publication → QA/Red Team → independent buyer/release verifier`.

Each contour owns a different question and may return FAIL even if the previous contour passed. A later editor/designer may not erase a method, claim boundary or buyer-value mechanism approved earlier. Cross-stage loss is a defect, not “simplification”.

P13 must review the **actual assembled artifact**, not only matrices/checklists, and return one consolidated repair list.

## PRODUCT CONTENT BLUEPRINT — mandatory for complex digital products

Before final long-form writing and before Codex/layout, create a complete block/component blueprint. For each major block capture as applicable:
- reader/user job and observable change after the block;
- selected methods/mechanics in plain Russian;
- why selected, limits and failure cases;
- metrics/formulas only where professionally meaningful;
- own tool/template/workbook action;
- book↔tool traceability;
- repeatable work → micro-regulation/knowledge base where useful;
- examples/cases/evidence;
- proof of completion/first useful result;
- future visual/interaction function.

Then produce the whole live text without losing the blueprint. After text/value freeze create the full visual specification/storyboard/asset+interaction map. Only then may Codex/technical production implement the frozen content. Codex may improve technical/UX execution within bounds but must not reinvent method, formulas, claims or product value.


## Content/Radar → Product Opportunity bridge

A raw founder/content idea may expose a paid product, but it enters Product Factory as a **candidate**, not a pre-approved SKU. Before creating a new hypothesis record, search existing product/portfolio/hypothesis objects and update the matching one when the problem/outcome is the same.

For candidate products born from creator/AI trends (for example “how to configure ChatGPT/AI as a reliable second brain”), explicitly research:
`current free tutorials / courses-books-templates / plugins-skills-agents / competing promises / customer complaints / setup burden / maintenance burden / what can be taught vs what requires hands-on implementation / DOKRUTI founder evidence / durable differentiation / format fit / legal-data-security risk`.

Do not sell “one evening and everything works” unless evidence supports it. A strong differentiator may be the tested operating system, failure map, setup sequence and verification method rather than generic prompt collections.

## Format challenger / technical production feedback

The Product Factory owns problem, outcome, method, promise and economics. A technical/UX executor is not a passive renderer: if implementation reveals that the frozen **carrier/interaction format** is materially weaker than another feasible route, it must raise a `FORMAT CHALLENGE` with 1–3 alternatives and, when useful, a bounded prototype.

It may improve interaction/UX/technical carrier within delegated rights. It may not silently change target audience, core methodology, public promise, price/economics or major product scope.

## Pain before format
Never decide «we need an e-book» before checking result, user context and economics. Format follows outcome and customer use.

## Svetlana practice check
Svetlana experience is high-value input, not automatic truth. For each significant practice:
`what she did → problem → professional analogue/evidence → strengths → limitations → improved version → status`.

External evidence may strengthen or correct a practice. Ask owner only when the choice changes the public method/claim materially.

## Competitive gap requirement
Product strategy must explicitly answer:
`what strong direct peers/products do well / what customers praise / what customers complain about / what result is missing / how DOKRUTI is materially better`.

A new product cannot be based only on our pre-existing idea without external discovery unless the owner explicitly requests a rapid internal prototype experiment.

## Donor synthesis
It is valid to study several strong competitor products and combine their transferable strengths into one stronger DOKRUTI product while fixing meaningful weaknesses. The synthesis must remain our own implementation and pass IP/legal review.

## E-book / long-form product quality
Before full writing establish:
- reader transformation;
- narrative spine;
- chapter dependency map;
- recurring case/story if useful;
- escalation of complexity;
- action cadence;
- visual plan;
- ending/implementation path.

Human-language rules:
- write as coherent prose, not repeated templates;
- complete natural sentences by default; no accidental slogan-fragments inside explanatory prose;
- vary rhythm/section structure when meaning allows;
- transitions explain why next section follows;
- examples feel operational, not generic;
- methods live inside the reader's problem;
- avoid overusing labels like «Блок / Методика / Пример заполнения» as mechanical scaffold;
- run full read-through + AI-smell lint before layout.

The full contract lives in [editorial-reader-experience.md](editorial-reader-experience.md).

Reader payoff test per major section:
`why continue / what changes in understanding / what can user do / how it connects to previous and next`.

## Visual production, not visual appendix
Art director enters before final production and remains responsible through export. Create real representative screens/pages on real content internally, but do not stop production merely to show a prototype unless an owner gate is genuinely required.

For owner-facing completion evidence, the final product must be inspectable visually in its real or near-real delivery format. A raw Markdown master is an internal source, not sufficient completion evidence when the product is meant to be visual. This does not create a mandatory owner approval gate.

## Production formats
HTML/e-book/workbook/dashboard/template/course/toolkit/interactive tool etc. No mandatory PDF/Excel/HTML default. Use the format that best delivers the result and can be reliably distributed.

## Product can be premium
Price follows outcome, alternatives, proof, completeness, distribution and economics — not file extension. Do not force low-ticket positioning.

## Canonical product root

One product uses one canonical root/master path. Research, methodology, production, visuals, QA and release artifacts may have subfolders by lifecycle/function, but not separate competing roots by executor (`Work`, `Codex`, editor, etc.). Temporary build/sandbox output must be promoted into the canonical root with readback before it can become release evidence.


## Required client package before release
As applicable, include and actually verify:
- START HERE/onboarding;
- main product;
- every promised workbook/tool/template/file;
- DEMO and CLEAN versions where the product requires examples;
- version/date consistency;
- license/usage boundaries;
- customer delivery/download route;
- support/update boundaries;
- product card/offer;
- audience/problem/result;
- contents and preview;
- FAQ/objections;
- launch content angles;
- related site topics/articles;
- metrics/experiment plan;
- legal/commercial state.

Create a factual RELEASE MANIFEST mapping every promise to an actual file/URL/version and QA result.

## Full-path customer QA
Test the actual route as a buyer:
`receive → open START HERE → understand first step → open main product → use one core tool/template → get first useful result → access all promised assets → understand next action/support/license`.

Open/render/execute real formats where technically possible. Filename existence is not functional evidence.

## Release package path / permission integrity

Verify the **exact final client package**, not only source files. For ZIP/bundled delivery, unpack the exact final archive into a clean directory and confirm every promised filename and literal relative link/href resolves to an existing intended target; Unicode/escaped-name corruption and orphan relative links are Critical.

Before READY/SELL, verify delivery permissions/ownership/integrity. Public `anyone=writer` or equivalent unnecessary write access is prohibited for client SEND ONLY artifacts. Use minimum necessary read/download access and confirm the buyer/third party cannot silently mutate the canonical product.


## Release gate
Any Critical or Major FAIL in methodology, narrative, art/UX, commercial, legal/IP, delivery, version consistency or technical production blocks release.

An `RC-*` filename is not evidence of readiness. Final state before reporting completion or requesting any genuine owner decision must pass independent verification against original acceptance with `Critical=0 / Major=0`.

## Research provenance vs customer-facing narrative
Research provenance is an internal production requirement, not the product's narrative spine. The customer-facing product should normally read as one coherent DOKRUTI solution, not as «мы взяли у компании A, затем у B».

However, preserve attribution/evidence where professionally or legally required: direct quotations, third-party figures/images, named/proprietary frameworks, licensed/open-source material, specific research/statistical claims or other content whose credibility/rights depend on source.

The goal is original synthesis, not concealed copying.

## Artifact architecture before file production

Before producing final files, map every planned artifact to a unique customer job. Merge/remove redundant files and format duplicates. Prefer one primary working environment plus supporting references only when they solve distinct jobs.

For workbooks define inputs, calculations, outputs, alerts, sheet relationships and first-use path before layout. Test positive, negative, boundary, empty and incorrect scenarios.

Use [product-experience-artifact-architecture.md](product-experience-artifact-architecture.md) and [universal-output-design.md](universal-output-design.md) for all client-facing artifacts, plus [publication-design-ebook.md](publication-design-ebook.md) when continuous long-form reading is part of the product.

## Product Value Integrity Gate — сильная идея не равна сильному продукту

Перед P11/P12 и повторно перед P14 оцени **сам конечный продукт**, а не качество исходной темы.

Обязательные доказательства:
1. `SEMANTIC PRESERVATION` — каждая сильная методика/механика/ограничение/неочевидный вывод из подтверждённого research/blueprint имеет место в финальном продукте или явно исключена с причиной. Потерянная критичная единица = блокер.
2. `VALUE DENSITY` — каждый существенный отрезок продукта добавляет новую полезную единицу: понимание, решение, пример, механизм, инструмент, критерий или действие. Повтор, перефразирование и декоративная длина не считаются ценностью.
3. `AHA / NOVELTY` — в продукте регулярно встречаются моменты «я этого не знал / не связывал / теперь вижу иначе», но они правдивы и применимы, а не сенсационны ради эффекта.
4. `FREE-AI REPLACEABILITY` — если обычный бесплатный запрос к универсальному ИИ даёт почти тот же результат без потери method/process/tools/proof, платный продукт слаб и возвращается в redesign.
5. `LONG-FORM CONTINUITY` — для 100–200+ страниц глубина первой/средней/последней трети сопоставима; нет деградации, повторов, потерянных нитей и mechanical chapter filling.
6. `COMPRESSION TEST` — если можно убрать большой фрагмент без потери результата, он требует сокращения/слияния.
7. `EXPANSION TEST` — если можно бесконечно добавить ещё 30–50 страниц без новой ценности, архитектура недостаточно дисциплинирована.
8. `BUYER-VALUE TEST` — человек понимает, за что платит в сравнении с бесплатной статьёй, видео, шаблоном, ChatGPT, наймом специалиста или бездействием.

Исполняемый эталон coverage/value-проверки: `scripts/product_value_gate.py`. Он не заменяет профессиональную редакторскую/покупательскую проверку, но не позволяет скрыть потерю методологии формальным «книга готова».

## Long-book production protocol

Для длинной книги сначала создай `METHOD LEDGER` с устойчивыми ID смысловых единиц. Затем:
`research/method ledger → full blueprint → chapter dependency map → whole draft → semantic coverage readback → value-density read-through → first/middle/end parity → Russian/Human Language → publication design → buyer/Red Team`.

В production trace для каждой обязательной смысловой единицы храни:
`method_id / source / why it matters / target chapter-or-tool / final location / evidence / status`.

Нельзя «очеловечивать» текст так, чтобы исчезли механика, ограничение, исключение, формула, причинная связь или граница применения. Если редактор меняет объяснение — смысловой ID должен оставаться покрытым.

Количество страниц не является acceptance. Хороший продукт может быть 40 или 240 страниц: размер следует нужной глубине.

## Calendar challenge rule

`7/10/30 дней`, марафон, чек-ин, streak и календарная программа не являются современным универсальным форматом и не добавляются ради видимости внедрения. Применяй их только если:
- результат объективно требует повторения/наблюдения во времени;
- есть понятный механизм, зачем нужен интервал;
- календарь снижает риск, а не создаёт лишнюю нагрузку;
- buyer experience выигрывает по сравнению с более простым first-value route.
## 200+ page production without owner chunk-management

A long product may legitimately be 100–200+ pages when the paid result requires that depth. Internal production may use bounded chunks for context safety, but Светлана must not become the chunk dispatcher. The Product Factory owns:
- one METHOD LEDGER and chapter dependency map for the whole product;
- continuity memory across chunks;
- automatic merge/reconciliation of repeated definitions, examples and transitions;
- semantic coverage readback after every major chunk;
- whole-book read-through after assembly;
- first/middle/last depth parity;
- one final voice and terminology system;
- final buyer-value review of the assembled product, not isolated chapters.

Do not stop at an outline, sample chapter or “эталонный фрагмент” when the accepted task is the complete book. If a technical limit requires staged generation, stage boundaries remain internal execution detail unless a true owner gate exists.


## Product mining from our own work

Product Factory получает кандидатов не только из внешнего Radar. После сильной внутренней реализации Business OS запускает [Knowledge Capital & Product Mining](knowledge-capital-product-mining.md): проблема → reusable method → proof → buyer pain → substitutes → possible carriers → cheap demand test. Новый SKU не создаётся автоматически.

## Proof-before-teach / own-use

Для продукта, который учит сайту, маркетингу, каналам, AI, аналитике или автоматизации, Product Council обязан спросить, какие элементы DOKRUTI реально применил у себя. Own-use не является абсолютным требованием для любой темы, но отсутствие доказательства снижает trust и должно быть честно отражено. См. [live-portfolio-proof.md](live-portfolio-proof.md).
