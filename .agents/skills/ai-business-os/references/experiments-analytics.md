# Experiments / Analytics / Learning Loop

## Status machine
`SIGNAL → RESEARCHED → PROPOSED → APPROVED FOR TEST → TESTING → TESTED → ADOPT / ADAPT / REJECT`.

`APPROVED FOR TEST` stores `approval_basis = POLICY / OWNER`. Business OS may approve a reversible, low-risk experiment inside an already accepted strategy, explicit budget/limits and existing permissions. Owner approval is required only for a true owner gate (material spend/strategy/public promise/legal-reputation/sensitive access/irreversible action).

## Test duration
Do not hardcode 7 days. Use mechanism:
- UI/operational test may be hours/days;
- paid traffic may need enough conversions;
- content/social may need several cycles;
- SEO may need weeks/months.

## Evidence discipline
Correlation/short spike is not automatically causal. Mark uncertainty.

## Learning write-back
Adopted methodology must be linked to experiment evidence; rejected tests remain searchable to avoid repeating failure.

Use [Learning Promotion / Self-Improving Business OS](learning-promotion-loop.md) to decide whether the learning belongs only to the current RUN, a dynamic channel/project playbook, or the stable Skill package. One trend or one successful creative is not enough to hardcode a permanent rule.

## Cross-channel data hub

When platform access/consent permits, Business OS should unify read-only evidence across site and active channels through IDs/UTM/events/export rather than copy metrics into many sheets. The analytical question is a path, not isolated vanity metrics: `published/discovered → consumed/retained → engaged/saved/shared → profile/site click → qualified message/order → revenue → repeat/unfollow`. Current Platform Cards define available metrics and freshness; missing data stays UNKNOWN.

## Artifact-level outcome evaluation | r9 FINAL

Architecture/string tests protect contracts but do not prove premium real-world output. For material task classes maintain representative artifact outcome evals from [Artifact Outcome Evals / Executor Graduation](artifact-outcome-executor-graduation.md).

An executor/plugin/model route may be adopted, expanded or demoted based on these results. Record total cost including owner rescue/rework, not only tool usage.

## Growth root-cause diagnostics

For content/channel tests, diagnose the first failed funnel stage before changing the artifact. Prefer one corrective hypothesis at a time: discovery, open/stop, retention, save/share/comment, follow/profile, return, qualified action, sale.

## Operational analytics

For material recurring workflows also inspect success/fail, latency, retries, human intervention, QA failure, publication/delivery and write-back integrity using [Automation Observability](automation-observability-external-capabilities.md). Business KPIs and workflow health are separate layers.
