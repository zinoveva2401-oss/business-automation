# Global Intelligence & Revenue Radar

## Mission
Continuously find signals that can improve `Докрути` or create money, then translate them into an actionable chain.

## Global Creative & Product Intelligence engine

This Radar is executed through [global-creative-product-intelligence.md](global-creative-product-intelligence.md). For broad scouting it must use a persistent Source Universe, separate geographic lanes, high-input→low-output funnel, seven donor types, viral-asset mining, trend velocity, Localization Arbitrage, audience-language mining, competitive whitespace, cross-industry transfer, Trend→DOKRUTI generation, Trend→Money translation, proactive experiments and DOKRUTI-specific learning.

Do not create a new standalone Radar/Signals/Ideas table if the current Business System, Content System, competitor/reference register, product-hypothesis bank, experiment log or decision register can hold the state.


## Radar domains

1. Customer pains/search/questions/reviews.
2. Competitor products, bundles, pricing, reviews, product launches.
3. International operator practices and case studies.
4. China/Asia and other markets with transferable mechanics.
5. Founder-led brands, positioning and thought leadership.
6. Content/SEO/social/video/distribution mechanics.
7. Affiliate/referral/partner opportunities.
8. B2B products, licensing, research, white-label, creative commerce.
9. AI/tools/automation/agents/plugins/connectors.
10. Visual/video/design emerging mechanics, hook structures, edit grammar, motion-carousel/animated-still/talking-head/demo patterns and channel packaging signals.
11. Law, advertising, tax, payment/platform changes.
12. New data sources and research methods.

## Signal card

Every strong signal:
- ID/date/source;
- what changed/what works;
- evidence and freshness;
- donor type: PRODUCT / OFFER / CONTENT / FORMAT / HOOK / BUSINESS MODEL / DISTRIBUTION;
- trend velocity + evidence window: EMERGING / ACCELERATING / MAINSTREAM / SATURATED / DECLINING / UNKNOWN;
- geography / global-traction evidence / explicit RU-gap status;
- audience-language evidence when relevant;
- money routes / NO MONETIZATION FIT;
- mechanism;
- why it may matter to DOKRUTI;
- where exactly to apply: product/content/site/channel/process/financial model/skill;
- concrete artifact idea;
- 1-day no/low-budget proof;
- appropriate validation test;
- metric;
- cost/risk;
- owner gate;
- decision: TAKE / ADAPT / TEST / HOLD / REJECT.

## Mandatory chain translation
Do not stop at «interesting case». For strong international donors the minimum translation is `SIGNAL → MECHANIC → RU GAP → DOKRUTI ADAPTATION → TEST`. For top donors also run the internal 10–30 application generator and money-route pass before shortlisting.
Examples of required translations:
- signal → 5 specific article angles + product hypothesis + website block;
- new tool → article comparison + affiliate candidate + internal workflow test;
- competitor product → gap map + our product concept;
- platform change → exact content/process/site adjustment;
- new visual mechanism → prototype type + content use case + QA test.

## Daily output
Only 3–7 strong NEW/meaningfully changed signals. No filler. This is the **output after broad scouting**, not permission to inspect only 3–7 sources. Under sufficient budget use the r9 funnel `100+ observations → ~30 candidates → ~10 donors → 3–7 signals → 1–3 tests`; if broad coverage is materially smaller, state the coverage gap.
Then max 1–3 proposed actions for the next 7 days.

For creative/media signals, do not send “this format is trending” alone. Translate it into:
`mechanic / source+date / why attention or comprehension may improve / target platform+audience / DOKRUTI test asset / production route / metric / kill rule`.

A visually impressive example with no transferable mechanic is not a strong Radar signal.

## Continuous competitor channel

Competitor intelligence is a permanent substream of Radar, not a one-time TOP list. On applicable daily/regular runs:
- discover new materially relevant founder-led direct peers;
- update already known peers only when product/price/funnel/content/positioning/review evidence meaningfully changed;
- track concrete SKUs, offers, launches, content/video topics that show observable traction, customer praise and complaints;
- classify large platforms separately as BENCHMARK/SUBSTITUTE/PARTNER unless they truly meet direct-peer criteria;
- convert meaningful change into `competitor → mechanic/gap → DOKRUTI implication → test/action`, without flooding the owner with unchanged profiles.

Store/update findings in the canonical competitor register when available. Daily Radar surfaces changes; deep Work research expands the register.

## Persistence
Radar result should be saved independently of whether Светлана is online at 08:00. A missed notification is not a reason to discard the report.

## Owner control
Radar cannot silently rewrite strategy or launch a materially new public/commercial commitment. A strong signal may become an internal WORK/EXP/CONT research or reversible test automatically when it stays inside accepted strategy, WIP, permissions and budget. Owner decision is required only at a true owner gate; a signal alone must not create owner micromanagement.

## Reliability protocol for scheduled radar
- one canonical radar automation, not overlapping duplicates;
- exact schedule in owner timezone;
- idempotent daily report keyed by date;
- if today's report already exists, update/append rather than duplicate;
- task run must save result to agreed store when connector allows;
- delivery notification and report persistence are separate concerns;
- failures logged with date/cause/next retry;
- weekly health check: last successful run, duplicates, write failures, stale sources.

## Delivery vs execution resilience

Scheduled radar execution must not depend on Светлана's phone, VPN or app being online at the scheduled minute. If the scheduling platform is server-side, device connectivity affects only when she reads/receives the result, not whether research should run.

Persistence priority:
1. canonical Business System row when write works;
2. pending spreadsheet comment / task artifact when cell write is blocked;
3. automation run output retained for later reading.

Never silently drop a completed radar because notification delivery failed.

## Novelty / Future Filter — не присылать «интересное», присылать преимущество

Сигнал не попадает к Светлане только потому, что он новый или популярен. Показывай его, если доказана хотя бы одна полезность:
- денежная возможность;
- новый сильный customer behavior/problem;
- transferable creative/distribution mechanic;
- технологический рычаг, экономящий время/деньги;
- конкурентный разрыв;
- продуктовая/сервисная возможность;
- важное право/платформенное изменение;
- ранний сигнал с потенциальным 6–24-месячным эффектом.

Для каждого сильного сигнала добавь горизонт:
`СЕЙЧАС / 6 МЕСЯЦЕВ / 12 МЕСЯЦЕВ / 24 МЕСЯЦА`, confidence и самый дешёвый способ проверить раньше конкурентов.

Утренний Radar должен отвечать не «что произошло в мире ИИ», а `какое преимущество может получить DOKRUTI и что реально сделать с этим сигналом`.

Визуально/креативно красивый тренд без переносимой механики, измеримой гипотезы или конкретного DOKRUTI-прототипа отбрасывается.

## Radar → learning / portfolio / product capital

Сильный сигнал после проверки должен быть направлен не только в контент. Проверь 4 назначения: `операционное улучшение / live-portfolio proof / knowledge asset / product-service hypothesis`. Если он связан с площадкой, сначала обнови её Platform Card. Если связан с уже решённой DOKRUTI задачей, проверь Knowledge Capital scan и существующий банк гипотез.


## AI / creator / personal opportunity radar

Кроме бизнес-сигналов, периодически проверяй новые безопасные способы применения ChatGPT/Plugins/AI для самого владельца: обучение, камера/речь, дети/репетиторство, устройства, документы, фото/видео, бытовая цифровая рутина. Используй `personal-ai-life-sysadmin-advisor.md`.

Для creator/video сигналов проверяй официальные trend/analytics/creative surfaces, собственную аналитику и специализированные инструменты. Найденный тренд переводится в `механика → почему работает → применимость → оригинальный тест → метрика`, а не в команду «копировать». См. `raw-footage-editing-trend-bank.md`.


## r8 donor-mechanics bridge to Content Factory

Radar may remain a separate scheduled research process, but it is not a separate owner workflow or knowledge silo. Strong content/creative signals should be written into the current Content System or existing reference/playbook object and become optional enrichment for the next Content Factory run.

For creative signals store the transferable layer: `source + date → observed mechanic → why it may work → conditions → DOKRUTI adaptation → rights/claim risk → cheapest test`. Do not transfer protected expression 1:1. A new international signal does not automatically displace an existing planned content topic; Content Factory decides priority against the live queue, business priorities and analytics.
