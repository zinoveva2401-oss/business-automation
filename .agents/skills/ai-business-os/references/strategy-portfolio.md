# CEO / Strategy / Portfolio Architecture

## Mission

Keep DOKRUTI coherent as a company, not a pile of unrelated products and experiments.

## Strategic questions

For material choices evaluate:
- target customer/problem territory;
- why DOKRUTI can credibly win;
- asset created: audience, search equity, IP, data, distribution, product library, partner network, brand trust;
- time to evidence and time to cash;
- scalability and dependence on Svetlana;
- fit with current MAIN and capital constraints;
- what must be stopped/not started if this is chosen.

## Portfolio logic

Do not optimize one product in isolation. Balance:
- cash-now experiments;
- evergreen digital assets;
- authority/content assets;
- repeat/recurrent revenue;
- strategic options for later SaaS/corporate solutions.

A new idea must have a place in the portfolio or be parked.

## Strategy gate

A material strategic proposal should state:
`current state → strategic problem/opportunity → options → evidence → economics → risks → reversible test → recommendation → what we stop → owner decision`.

Do not confuse a trend with strategy.


## Commercial architecture master

Commercial architecture is a cross-functional master, not an ad-hoc sales note. When the live master registry says it is `PARTIAL / IN WORK`, downstream work may use only the verified slices and must mark missing links as `UNKNOWN / PENDING`; it may not silently invent a complete funnel or create a duplicate commercial master.

The canonical commercial map should connect, as applicable:
`segment / buyer-user-payer / pain-job / product-service-B2B-partner offer / value+proof / price+economics / acquisition channel / content→next step / funnel-customer journey / checkout-payment / delivery-first value / CRM+data+consent / legal-tax dependencies / attribution-KPI / repeat-expansion`.

Product, Content, Growth, Site and Sales changes that materially alter this chain must read the current commercial master first and write confirmed deltas back to its canonical source or Business System state. A local executor may not declare the whole commercial architecture finished merely because its own component is ready.

## Knowledge capital flywheel

Стратегический портфель включает не только текущие SKU, но и доказанные интеллектуальные активы: methods, templates, datasets, Skills, automation recipes, case evidence, content franchises, product hypotheses. После material VERIFIED work запускай Knowledge Capital scan; сохраняй кандидатов в существующий канонический банк, не создавая новый объект без distinct value core.

## Market whitespace / do-not-go challenge

Перед новым коммерческим направлением стратегия обязана рассмотреть не только «можем ли мы», но и «стоит ли сюда входить». Проверка: density of direct peers/substitutes, buyer urgency, price/proof, differentiation, acquisition difficulty, delivery economics, founder fit, capital created and adjacent whitespace.

Если поле переполнено и преимущество DOKRUTI не доказано — предложи соседний job/segment/format/channel, где тот же capability создаёт больше edge. Доход не прогнозировать точной цифрой без demand+price+volume+capacity model.
