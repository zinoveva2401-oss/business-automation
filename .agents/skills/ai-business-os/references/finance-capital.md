# Capital Builder + CFO

## Separate roles

### Capital Builder
Long horizon, asset creation, leverage, opportunity cost, scalability, founder time, optionality.

### CFO
Numbers and forecast: cashflow, P&L, scenarios, unit economics, breakeven, runway, plan-fact, investment gate.

### Accountant/Tax
See legal-accounting-ip-ru.md.

## Financial gate before a significant initiative

Minimum model:
- purpose/revenue stream;
- one-time build cost;
- recurring cost;
- owner hours and imputed cost;
- variable costs/commissions/tax assumptions;
- price/revenue unit;
- LOW / BASE / HIGH demand scenario;
- break-even units/revenue;
- cash timing;
- time to first revenue;
- payback window;
- downside / max acceptable loss;
- stop rule;
- scale rule.

If numbers are unknown, use explicit assumptions and label them. Do not create fake precision.

## Business finance loop

`forecast → approve budget → execute → capture actuals → P&L by stream → variance → corrective action`.

Every revenue stream should eventually show:
`revenue / refunds / commissions / taxes / variable costs / allocated fixed costs / owner hours / contribution margin / next decision`.

## Founder time

Owner time is a scarce economic input. Compare routes not only by cash outlay but by hours and cognitive load.

## Portfolio logic

Do not rely on one monetization mode. Score each revenue lane by:
- strategic fit;
- speed to first cash;
- gross margin;
- recurring potential;
- owner-time intensity;
- scalability;
- trust/brand effect;
- legal/tax complexity;
- operational maintenance;
- evidence of demand.

## Cash-first in constrained period

When liquidity is weak, prioritize tests with low sunk cost and short evidence cycle, while still building reusable assets.
