# Research / Competitive Intelligence / Parsers

## Research intent lock

Before search, define the research job: `DISCOVERY / HYPOTHESIS TEST / CONTINUOUS MONITORING / SKU SELECTION / DONOR REVERSE ENGINEERING`. Do not silently turn discovery into proof of an idea we already like.

## Direct-peer definition and taxonomy

Before naming competitors, define why they compete with DOKRUTI. Classify every relevant entity:

- `DIRECT PEER` — comparable founder-led expert/small expert business competing for similar customer problem, attention and spend;
- `ADJACENT` — overlaps in problem or audience but sells a materially different category;
- `SUBSTITUTE` — alternative way the customer solves the same job;
- `BENCHMARK` — useful large/strong operator for mechanics, not a direct commercial peer;
- `PARTNER` — potentially complementary or referral-capable.

For DOKRUTI direct competitor research, prioritize real people/founder-led expert businesses with content/speaking + digital products/services + their own funnel. Corporations and large platforms are not direct peers by default.

## Continuous competitive intelligence

Do not stop at an arbitrary TOP-10. Build/update a living register of all materially relevant peers discovered over time. Track changes in concrete products, prices, offers, funnels, content formats, strong-performing topics/videos where evidenced, reviews, complaints and positioning. Reuse the register across chats when the canonical store is available.

## Competitor/Product Reverse Engineering

For product creation, study enough strong concrete products/SKUs to reach mechanism saturation; this may be 5, 10, 20 or another justified number. More is useful only while new evidence/mechanics/gaps continue to appear.

For each product capture:
- seller / direct-peer class;
- target customer/job;
- pain and desired result;
- exact product/SKU and price/business model;
- promise and proof;
- actual public contents/modules;
- user journey/onboarding;
- tools/templates/functions;
- delivery format;
- sales/distribution/funnel;
- recurring/upsell element;
- reviews/praise;
- complaints/friction;
- missing outcome;
- likely buying reason, clearly labeled if inferred;
- manual/expert workload vs scalable delivery;
- what to adapt;
- what to reject;
- source/date/evidence quality.

Do not infer paid/closed contents you have not accessed. Use public evidence, purchased/authorized materials, demos, reviews and legitimate sources.

## Discovery before fit

For a new product, first map what customers already buy and complain about. Only after that compare findings with Svetlana's competence, DOKRUTI positioning and product strategy. Existing internal ideas are hypotheses, not the research answer.

## Customer Voice

Mine where legally/technically allowed:
- reviews;
- search queries;
- comments/forums/communities;
- customer support questions;
- public product reviews;
- public complaints;
- sales objections.

Preserve the buyer's actual language internally. Do not reproduce long copyrighted passages in outputs. Separate direct evidence from analyst inference.

## Evidence-based ranking

No pseudo-precision. A score is allowed only when the underlying dimensions have evidence. Missing demand/sales/economic evidence remains `UNKNOWN / INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE`; do not convert unknowns into 5/5 or 24/25.

Useful sales proxies when direct sales data is unavailable may include repeated promotion, dedicated sales pages, review volume/recency, customer examples, cohort/relaunch evidence, recurring versions, upsells, community activity or other concrete observable signals. Label them as proxies, not sales facts.

## Product synthesis

`strong donor mechanics + customer praise + competitor weaknesses/complaints + verified Svetlana practice + external operator/professional evidence → improved original DOKRUTI solution`.

It is valid to combine complementary strong mechanics from multiple products and intentionally fix their weaknesses. The goal is to be materially more useful, clearer, easier to apply or more complete — not merely different.

## Donor / adaptation boundary

Aggressive benchmarking is encouraged. We may study and adapt:
- product architecture;
- composition of a bundle;
- functional/user-flow mechanics;
- onboarding;
- funnel structure;
- price ladder logic;
- content formats/hooks at the idea/mechanic level;
- information architecture;
- feature classes and workflow patterns.

Do not copy protected expression 1:1: copyrighted text, proprietary code, distinctive branded visual composition, trademarks presented as ours, private/licensed files, closed paid materials without authorization, or other protected expression.

Internal provenance remains traceable.

## External operator practice

We may learn from executives, large companies and international experts beyond Svetlana's direct experience. Classify origin:
- `SVETLANA PRACTICE`;
- `EXTERNAL OPERATOR PRACTICE`;
- `RESEARCH/STANDARD`;
- `DOKRUTI SYNTHESIS`;
- `DOKRUTI TESTED`.

Public text must not misrepresent external practice as Svetlana's personal execution history.

## Parsers / data harvesting

Use APIs/exports first where available. Web parsing must respect access controls, terms, robots/rate limits, privacy and law. No bypass of authentication, paywalls, anti-bot or platform safeguards.

Parser output needs:
`source/url / date / fields / dedupe rule / freshness / error handling / storage / legal constraint`.

## Provenance register

Internal evidence must remain traceable even if final product is synthesized without donor list.

## Research completion gate

Research cannot be labeled complete if a critical part of its own acceptance is postponed to a future plan. Before handoff check:
`intent answered / direct peers correct / SKU evidence sufficient / customer voice gathered / unknowns labeled / contradictions resolved / decision usefulness / provenance / acceptance complete`.

## No originality theater

The system does not need to invent a brand-new management law to create value. A product may be valuable because DOKRUTI finds strong existing practices, compares them, removes friction, combines complementary mechanisms, adapts them to the target user's reality, adds verified Svetlana practice where relevant and turns the synthesis into an easier-to-use finished solution.

Originality means original selection, synthesis, explanation, tools, examples, user journey and implementation — not pretending established ideas were invented by us.

## Domain-expertise expansion

When a topic exceeds Svetlana's verified operating experience, deliberately research credible external operators, standards, professional bodies and evidence from the relevant domain. Use that material to strengthen the solution while preserving truthful provenance and avoiding unsupported personal-authority claims.

## Whitespace research

Конкурентный анализ должен уметь ответить не только «кто сильнее», но и:
- какие jobs/segments/formats уже commoditized;
- где много предложения, но complaints/outcomes остаются незакрытыми;
- какие adjacent jobs растут, но имеют мало сильных direct peers;
- где новая capability/technology lowers delivery cost;
- какой smallest test distinguishes real whitespace from analyst fantasy.

`малоконкурентно` не равно `есть спрос`. Обязательна отдельная проверка demand/proxy/economics.
