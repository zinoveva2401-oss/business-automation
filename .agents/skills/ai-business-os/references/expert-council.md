## Trigger matrix

### Strategic portfolio change
Required: CEO/Strategy + Internal Business Analyst + Capital Builder + CFO + CRO. Add Brand/Product/Legal/Tech depending on the decision.

### New product
Required: Product Strategist + Research/Competitive Intelligence + Customer Voice + Internal Business Analyst + CFO + CRO/Commercial + Marketing/Growth + Legal/IP + Product UX/Artifact Architecture + QA/Red Team.
For intellectual/long-form products also activate: Domain Methodologist + Narrative/Book Architect + Human-Language/Reader-Experience Editor + Semantic-Preservation Auditor + Value-Density/AHA Reviewer + Skeptical Buyer/Free-AI Challenger + Publication/Art/Information Design + independent Editorial verifier.
The Research contour must perform direct-peer discovery and concrete product-donor reverse engineering before product architecture is frozen. The Semantic-Preservation Auditor traces critical method IDs from research/blueprint into the final artifact. The Skeptical Buyer compares the actual product with free AI, free content, software, consultant/hire and do-nothing alternatives. The Art/UX contour remains active through final visual production, not only prototype advice. The QA contour includes independent completion verification.
Conditional: Accounting/Tax, Tech, Video, Security, B2B production, SEO.

### Article/content campaign / raw founder idea
Required: Marketing Matrix + Research/Analyst + Editorial + Content + Creative Direction + Distribution + Brand/Art + Measurement.
Conditional: Hook/Retention, Video Producer/Director/Editor, Motion/Sound, Product/CRO, Affiliate, Legal, SEO/AEO.
For raw owner ideas the council first decides whether the strongest route is article/video/carousel/tool/product opportunity/no-action; it does not simply obey the first format mentioned.

### New revenue channel
Required: CRO + CFO + Capital Builder + Marketing + Sales + Legal/Tax + Brand Trust + Analytics.

### Brand/trademark/domain/IP
Required: Strategy + Brand + Russian Legal/IP + Marketing. Add CFO for material spending and technical/domain specialist for implementation.

### Personal/Founder development or worldview content
Required: Founder OS + Christian worldview + evidence/behavior design + Brand/Content. Add legal/reputation only if public claim/risk exists.

### Site/code/automation
Required: Task owner + Tech Architect + Security/Data + QA. Add Marketing/UX/SEO for public surfaces; CFO for paid infra; Legal for personal data/payment/adtech.

### CRM / sales funnel / attribution
Required: Sales/CRO + Marketing + Data/BI/Attribution + Customer Success. Add Legal/Data for personal data; Accounting/CFO for payment/refund/revenue reconciliation.

### Automation/reliability incident
Required: AI/Automation + Reliability/Local IT + Security/Data + task owner. Add vendor/platform specialist when external service is the suspected failure.

### Skill/tool architecture change
Required: Skill/Capability Architect + AI Benchmark + Security/Data + QA + Knowledge/SoT. Add CFO when cost/limits are material.

### Financial/accounting question
Required: CFO + Accounting/Tax. Add Legal if classification/contract/collection/rights are involved.

### B2B merch / corporate design / physical production
Required: CRO + Brand/Marketing + Art/UX + Procurement/Vendor/Production + CFO + Legal/IP. Add Accounting/Tax for invoicing/commissions and Logistics only if DOKRUTI actually takes fulfillment responsibility.

### Reputation / public statement / speaking
Required: Founder OS + Brand/PR + Content. Add Legal when claims, advertising, disputes or third-party rights create risk; add Research when factual claims must be evidenced.

### Team / HR / employment product or advice
Required: People/HR + Russian Legal/Labor + Product/Research. Add CFO/Operations when staffing economics or process design is involved.

### Multi-domain owner question
If a user question materially touches three or more domains, automatically form the smallest sufficient council. Светлана should not need to say «перепроверь юристом/финансистом/маркетологом».

## Council protocol

1. Each activated contour answers only its own risk/question.
2. Identify conflicts.
3. Resolve by source hierarchy, evidence, reversibility, economics and owner values.
4. Give one integrated recommendation.
5. Ask owner only when choice changes strategy, money/risk materially, public identity or irreversible implementation.

If real subagents exist, parallel execution is still NOT the default for Svetlana/Codex. Use sequential independent passes unless Business OS has justified the extra quota/risk reduction and Svetlana explicitly approves the additional spawned task. Technical availability alone is not authorization.

## Long-form / publication trigger

For any substantial e-book, manual, guide, research report or long article, automatically activate:
`Narrative Architect + Human-Language Editor + Reader-Experience Reviewer + Publication Designer/Typographer + Information Design + Art/UX + independent Editorial verifier`.

## Visual concept / design trigger

For a new or materially redesigned visual system, automatically activate:
`Brand + Visual Research + Art Director + UX/Information Design + Visual Systems Designer + Independent Design Critic`.
Do not let the same production pass be the sole visual approver.

## Product toolkit/workbook trigger

For a workbook/toolkit/customer package, automatically activate:
`Product + Customer Voice + Product UX/Onboarding + Artifact Architecture + Spreadsheet/Technical specialist + Human-Language + Art/Information Design + QA`.
The council must test usefulness and buyer comprehension, not merely formulas/file existence.


## Universal output-design trigger

For every meaningful owner-facing, customer-facing or public artifact where presentation affects comprehension, action, trust or perceived value, activate a lightweight `Communication/Production Design + Information Design` pass. Depth follows risk: a single text post does not need a full art council, while a product package, presentation, workbook, report, landing or campaign does.

Specialized triggers stack on top:
- long-form → Publication Designer/Typographer;
- new visual direction → Art Director + Independent Design Critic;
- workbook/dashboard → Product UX + Spreadsheet specialist;
- presentation/commercial proposal → Communication Designer + Commercial/CRO reviewer;
- social/carousel → Content + Brand/Art + channel-native composition review.


## Video / motion / audio trigger

For substantial time-based media activate:
`Content/Narrative + Creative Director/Hook + Art Director + Video Producer/Director/Editor + Motion + Sound/Music + Audio/Caption QA + channel/platform reviewer + independent Media verifier`.
Use `creative-content-production.md` + the dedicated Media Production Quality contract; render success alone is not approval. If Svetlana records new footage, include the Founder Shoot Card before capture.

## Director-mode consortium — think ahead, not only review behind

For material business decisions the council must include only applicable directors, but each activated director has a forward-looking obligation before execution.

Minimum director questions:
- `CEO/Strategy`: what portfolio asset and dependency does this create; what changes over 12–24 months?
- `CRO/Commercial`: where does demand become money; what is the repeat/upsell/partner route; what breaks the offer?
- `CFO/Capital`: margin after real owner hours/fees/taxes/refunds; cash timing/downside/opportunity cost.
- `Legal/Accounting`: rights, data, advertising, contract, tax, responsibility and future migration/rework risk.
- `COO`: first 10/100 customers, exceptions, capacity, recovery, handoff.
- `Product`: why pay instead of free AI/free content/hire/do nothing; first value; retention/usefulness.
- `Marketing/Creative`: attention/proof/distribution/format; can weak idea be rescued honestly?
- `Technology/AI`: capability, security, reversibility, lock-in, maintenance.

Before an owner gate, merge disagreements into one concise decision brief: `что не видим / риски / stronger route / recommendation / what would change our mind`.

A consortium that only confirms the producer is not independent thinking.
