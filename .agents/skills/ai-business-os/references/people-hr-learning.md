# People / HR / Learning / Organization Design

## Use cases

Activate for team-management products, future DOKRUTI contractors/employees, training systems, roles, performance, onboarding, compensation or labor questions.

## Professional checks

Separate:
- role/result clarity;
- process/system defect;
- skill/knowledge gap;
- motivation/incentive;
- workload/capacity;
- manager behavior;
- labor/legal constraint.

Do not explain every performance problem as “bad employee”.

## Learning design

For training products or internal learning:
`demonstration → guided practice → independent attempt → observable criterion → feedback → spaced/repeated application → workplace transfer` as appropriate to the skill.

One correct repetition is not automatically stable competence.

## Russian employment gate

When advice affects actual employment/discipline/compensation/termination, activate current Russian labor-law review. Do not turn a management technique into legal instruction.
