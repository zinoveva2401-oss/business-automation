# Revenue Portfolio / Partnerships / B2B Creative Commerce

## Revenue Radar categories
Evaluate periodically:

1. Premium digital books/toolkits/workbooks.
2. Bundles/libraries/memberships when there is repeat value.
3. Corporate licenses/team packs.
4. Affiliate/referral/recommendation revenue.
5. Paid benchmark/research/industry reports.
6. White-label/co-branded intellectual products.
7. B2B creative commerce.
8. Paid workshops/recorded learning/keynotes.
9. Sponsorship/native commercial collaborations compatible with trust.
10. Marketplaces for templates/agents/tools.
11. Dashboards/AI assistants/automation packs.
12. SaaS later, after demand/capital/support readiness.

## Candidate score
`audience fit / pain / revenue potential / speed to cash / margin / owner hours / scale / recurring / asset creation / brand trust / legal-tax / operational load / evidence`.

## Affiliate/Referral
For each program capture:
- official source;
- product relevance;
- commission model;
- cookie/attribution window if public;
- payout threshold/method/currency/geography;
- Russia/owner tax-regime compatibility;
- disclosure/advertising rules;
- brand trust risk;
- best content/use cases;
- LOW/BASE/HIGH revenue scenario;
- test/stop rule.

Do not use a service only because it pays commission.

## B2B Creative Commerce
Offer can be intellectual package rather than manufacturing:
- corporate apparel/form design;
- merch capsule;
- POSM;
- launch/event kit;
- retail/navigation/signage;
- social/brand asset system;
- presentation/sales materials;
- production brief/specification.

Workflow:
`client problem → commercial scope → brand/marketing → art/fashion/graphic → production constraints → procurement/vendor options → costing/margin → legal/IP → mockups → technical production package`.

Avoid taking inventory/logistics/production liability by default unless economics and contracts justify it.

## Licensing / White label
Design terms for scope, territory, users, duration, modification rights, attribution, updates/support and IP ownership. Legal review before launch.

## Physical-production boundary

By default `Докрути` sells the intellectual/creative package, specification and production-ready assets. It does not silently assume inventory, manufacturing, fulfillment, product-safety or logistics liability.

If Светлана chooses to take production/fulfillment responsibility, activate Procurement/Vendor + CFO + Accounting/Tax + Legal/IP + Logistics/Risk and model margin, defects, samples, minimum order, payment schedule, delivery, returns and responsibility before quoting.

## Additional revenue lanes worth periodic radar checks

Do not force them, but periodically consider:
- corporate research/benchmark subscriptions;
- sponsored research/content only with trust/disclosure fit;
- certification/licensing of DOKRUTI materials when a real standard emerges;
- paid speaking/keynotes/event partnerships;
- curated vendor/service directories with transparent monetization;
- corporate onboarding/training kits sold by seat/team/license;
- data/benchmark products built from lawfully obtained aggregated data;
- implementation partner ecosystem where partners deliver work and DOKRUTI licenses/educates, subject to contract/tax/legal review.
