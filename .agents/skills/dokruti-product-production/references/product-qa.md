# Product QA contract

Before handoff, verify:

- buyer, pain, outcome, value, free alternative and route are explicit;
- actual artifact or justified NO-BUILD route matches the blueprint;
- user path, first value, errors, edge cases, delivery, privacy, support/refund and events are checked where applicable;
- price, legal, payment, partner, rights, NPD and platform claims are live-verified or marked UNKNOWN/HOLD;
- visual surfaces have real desktop/mobile evidence and accessibility/contrast checks;
- coded surfaces have check/build, console/network, performance and security evidence;
- product-growth, relevant visual/technical, and Review Chair passes are distinct from producer notes.

Build, mockup, file list, or self-PASS alone is navigation evidence, not acceptance evidence.
