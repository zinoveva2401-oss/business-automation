# Content QA contract

Acceptance evidence must cover:

- owner intent, audience/pain, role, master format, payoff, next step and channel fit;
- factual claims, product/service/commercial truth and claim ledger;
- human editorial quality, structure, readability, accessibility and exact author meaning where applicable;
- visual/media asset role, provenance, rights, captions/transcript, master/delivery and actual inspection where applicable;
- SEO/schema/links/metadata and responsive renderer result where applicable;
- commercial disclosure/legal status, AI-data safety, privacy and publication boundary;
- relevant specialist passes and Review Chair on the exact final package.

Draft copy, generated metadata, platform export, or self-PASS alone is not final evidence.
