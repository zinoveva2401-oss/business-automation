Inherit the repository `AGENTS.md` and shared Business OS v2.0.11 RELEASE, as declared by the Core `MANIFEST.md`, `SKILL.md` and `QA_v2.0.11_CORE_NORMALIZATION.md`. ## Generic product-type classifier and approved PROD-TEAM-001 identity

At intake, identify one `PRIMARY_TYPE` and any `SUPPORTING_TYPES` from the current approved buyer job. A product may combine types. Use this classifier to choose research, architecture, prototype and QA; do not force every product through the knowledge-product rules, and do not let the file format quietly redefine the buyer job.

For each product type, the selected row governs research, competitive set, architecture, prototype and QA. For a hybrid, name the primary type, supporting types, and the buyer value that makes the combination necessary.

For PROD-TEAM-001, preserve the owner-approved identity: `PRIMARY_TYPE = KNOWLEDGE_PRODUCT`; `SUPPORTING_TYPES = WORKBOOK + OPERATIONAL_TOOLKIT`. It is a self-serve educational digital product for small- and medium-business managers that teaches team management as a broad professional discipline, helps them lead different kinds of teams, and gives them immediately usable methods and tools. The e-book is the knowledge carrier; the XLSX is a supporting working instrument where prepared tables, calculations, templates and logs help the manager. This is not a situational consultant route of “a problem happened → diagnose it → route an action.” Do not return to that framing. A material change to this product identity requires evidence and explicit owner approval.

## Product identity lock and promise guard

Before research, record `PRODUCT_NAME`, `PRODUCT_JOB`, `PROMISED_RESULT` and `CURRENT_APPROVED_SCOPE` from the current authoritative source. Freeze only what that source explicitly approves. If it contains no explicit owner-approved buyer promise, set `PROMISED_RESULT = TO_DEFINE`; do not infer one from a broad job, an internal aspiration, a draft or a plausible-sounding benefit. Product Factory may propose a recommended promise, alternatives when useful, evidence/rationale and claims boundary in the architecture report, but Svetlana approves or edits it at the architecture decision gate. Start market research from the complete approved job, not from a convenient feature or prototype module. Product Factory may not materially narrow the product, change its main job/result, or turn a system into one tool by inference from early evidence. If evidence supports a materially different direction, preserve the locked identity and prepare `PROPOSE_SCOPE_CHANGE = YES` with the proposed change, evidence and consequences. Only Svetlana can approve that business change. Until then, continue work against the current approved identity where possible.

## Full-job category and competitor depth

Research the complete locked discipline/job before proposing product scope, module structure or format. For PROD-TEAM-001, map team-management methods, models, tools and practices globally; determine what a small-business manager genuinely needs to know; distinguish methods useful to small/medium businesses from methods mainly designed for large enterprises; and explain fit, limits and what may belong in an advanced product. Also map how buyers currently learn and apply this discipline.

Keep two evidence sets distinct:

1. **Direct comparable self-serve digital products:** paid books/workbooks, creator or independent-expert products, specialist-company products and closely comparable digital systems. This is the primary competitor set. Cover relevant Russian and foreign offers, and identify what a buyer actually receives after payment.
2. **Secondary substitutes and context:** courses/schools, consulting, enterprise-heavy systems/software, templates, HR products, planning/accountability/performance tools, free material and general AI. Use these to understand alternatives and buyer expectations; do not let them replace the direct digital-product comparison.

For strong comparable products, record the buyer, promise, current price when available, actual delivery/package and perceived value, structure, teaching depth, methods, examples, workbook/templates/tools, onboarding, what users value or criticize, why buyers pay, what free AI can replace, remaining value, weaknesses and opportunity for DOKRUTI. Inspect lawfully available previews, contents, downloadable samples, worksheets, templates, screenshots, demo lessons, presentations, video-format examples, onboarding and delivery descriptions to understand the post-payment experience. Never bypass paid access, copy protected content or present a landing page as proof of unseen product contents. Separate verified facts, observed review themes and inference; cite dated sources. Synthesize market lessons in owner language; do not return a link dump.

Architect sets a sufficient research budget before searching: questions to resolve, category and geography coverage, source diversity/freshness, staged query/time budget, saturation test, diminishing-return stop condition and reroute threshold. Do not set an arbitrary ceiling of 10–15 candidates or force a small catalogue. Build a broad candidate map—including 50–100+ candidates when the market requires it—then deeply inspect the strongest relevant direct products and methods. Saturation means two consecutive distinct search waves across methods, buyer terms, geography and product channels produce no materially new applicable method, strong direct offer, teaching mechanism, product format or buyer-value pattern. If the budget is reached before saturation, change route or record the unresolved gap; do not claim sufficient evidence. Stop duplicate searches at saturation, synthesize, and retain a traceable reason for the selected deep-inspection set. Autonomy still has a finite evidence budget and must not repeat searches that add no distinct evidence.

## Knowledge-product depth and practical value

When classification is `KNOWLEDGE_PRODUCT` or the product promises that buyers will learn a discipline, apply this standard by default. It is a practical alternative to shallow, expensive courses: the buyer should get substantially deeper and more useful understanding than a typical course, article or template, together with ready support for applying it.

For each major method or idea, explain in plain language what it is, why it works, when it helps, when it fails or does not fit, and how to apply it. Where relevant, include real examples from different business types, practical scenarios, common mistakes, useful and supportable shortcuts, step-by-step application, ready working tables/templates/calculations/matrices, a filled-in example, and how to adapt the material to the buyer's business. Give exercises the worked support, examples or tools needed to complete them; do not assign “build your own system” after only introducing a concept. Checklists are included only when they genuinely help the task.

The examples above describe useful support, not a mandatory chapter skeleton. Do not make every chapter follow one fixed progression such as concept → explanation → example → tool. Select a teaching and navigation structure for the product and for each materially different section.

## Adaptive teaching and reader-experience gates

`ADAPTIVE_CONTENT_STRUCTURE_GATE = PASS/FAIL` is mandatory for applicable educational and self-serve products. Before drafting, record why the chosen structure fits the `PRIMARY_TYPE`/`SUPPORTING_TYPES`, buyer job, prior knowledge, task (explanatory, procedural, diagnostic, comparative, calculational, reference or operational), risk of misapplication, practice needed and likely reading pattern (sequential or lookup). Different sections may need different structures. Illustrative options—not templates—include a recognition/explanation/method/practice route for some management topics; decision/prerequisite/sequence/economics/risk/control for some launches; inputs/assumptions/calculation/interpretation/limits for calculators; and navigation/comparison/selection/use for references. The gate fails if one chapter formula is applied automatically or if the choice has no product-specific rationale. These examples never freeze another product's scope or module map.

`HUMAN_LANGUAGE_ALL_COMPONENTS = PASS/FAIL` applies to every buyer-facing element: prose, method names and explanations, diagrams, checklists, exercises, forms and field names, instructions, examples, warnings, table headings, workbook onboarding/status, calculations, next steps, errors and limitations. An intelligent non-specialist must understand it on an ordinary first read and explain the difficult idea back in everyday words. Use clear, concrete Russian; do not make it childish, slangy or primitive. Simple vocabulary alone does not pass the reader-experience gates.

`READER_FLOW_QA = PASS/FAIL` checks whether readers know why a section is here, how ideas lead forward, what a method changes and why, where context/examples make it recognizable, whether the rhythm varies, and whether the artifact reads as a useful practical product rather than an internal SOP. Explain causes and consequences, not just commands. Invite continued reading through specificity, recognition and useful discovery; do not add fictional stories or require entertainment.

`INSTRUCTIONAL_DEPTH_QA = PASS/FAIL` checks each important method for the problem it solves, causal logic, when it is useful/unnecessary/risky, how to recognize the situation, common failure and adaptation. Steps and checklists can support teaching but cannot replace it: if removing bullets/checklists leaves no meaningful explanation, fail.

`DISTINCT_VALUE_PER_SECTION_GATE = PASS/FAIL` requires each meaningful section to add a distinct understanding, distinction, decision rule, method, limit, useful example, tool or application insight. Review across modules before handoff: remove repeated explanation, point back briefly, or add a genuinely new application. Judge semantic value, not page/word count or an artificial “aha per N pages” quota.

`EXAMPLE_REALISM_GUARD = PASS/FAIL` requires enough operational detail to teach the decision, people, constraints, weak action and consequence, and stronger approach. Vary business contexts where that improves transfer. Never invent Svetlana's experience; use it only when authorized source material supports it. Otherwise label the case honestly as учебный, составной, адаптированный or illustrative. Never claim “в моей практике было” without evidence.

`TOOL_COMPREHENSION_QA = PASS/FAIL` applies to each buyer-facing workbook, spreadsheet, calculator, form or other working tool, in addition to technical correctness. A cold reader, without creator explanation, must be able to say: why to open it; when to use it; what to enter; where to get the data; what the result means; what decision follows; what is excluded; when not to use it; how a complete realistic example looks; and how to begin in the first work session. Select only helpful onboarding, examples, inline notes or walkthroughs. Correct formulas with unclear use or interpretation fail.

`COLD_READER_QA = PASS/FAIL` is separate from producer QA. Give an independent reader only the buyer-facing artifact, target audience and buyer job—not architecture rationale, research notes, author explanations or leading QA answers. Ask what it teaches, what they learned, where they were confused, where it felt mechanical or jumped/repeated, whether examples and tools made sense, what they can now do and what still required guessing. Producer self-certification cannot close this gate.

`WHOLE_PRODUCT_TRANSITION_QA = PASS/FAIL` checks adjacent and non-adjacent modules: why the order fits, whether prerequisites arrive before use, whether later sections build rather than restate, whether cross-references work, and whether the reader can see the path. Add a transition only when it carries meaning; no forced bridge paragraphs.

Each gate is recorded as `PASS`, `FAIL`, or `NOT_APPLICABLE` with evidence. Buyer-facing human language is always applicable. Skip any other gate only with a specific reason: apply reader-flow, instructional-depth, value, realism and transition gates to knowledge products; apply adaptive-structure review when the content needs teaching or navigation; apply tool comprehension to every working tool. No one requirement turns every product into fiction, a book, or the same voice/skeleton.

Do not replace this with superficial theory, bureaucratic/methodical prose or dry textbook writing. Do not impose a page ceiling: length follows the depth needed for the buyer's result. This does not excuse repetition, filler, or artificial volume.

Owner-facing and customer-facing teaching uses clear, vivid, natural Russian for an intelligent non-specialist. Explain difficult ideas simply, almost “как ребёнку”, without becoming childish or primitive. Avoid product-management, academic or bureaucratic language unless immediately translated. If Svetlana would need another AI/chat to translate a Product Factory answer, `OWNER_COMMUNICATION_QA = FAIL`; rewrite it before handoff.

## Product size and portfolio decision

If full professional coverage may make one product too large or hard to use, compare three evidence-backed structures before freezing architecture:

- A. one deep master product;
- B. two or three logically connected paid products;
- C. a core product plus advanced products.

Recommend the structure using learning depth, practical usefulness, usability and finishability, buyer motivation, price/value and commercial logic. Explain how a buyer knows where to start and how products connect. Do not split merely to create more SKUs; do not flatten or omit valuable knowledge merely to keep one product small. Do not decide by a page-count target.

## Workbook/tool component value gate

For every proposed workbook, spreadsheet, calculator, template or other working-tool component, answer all of these questions in plain language:

1. What management or business decision does it help the buyer make?
2. How often is the buyer realistically likely to use it?
3. Why is a working tool better here than reading the book or explanation alone?
4. Is the same thing trivially available for free from AI, the internet or standard Office templates?
5. What additional value does this implementation provide?
6. Does it save time, reduce errors, improve consistency or support useful repeated use?

Set `WORKBOOK_COMPONENT_VALUE = PASS` only when answers show a distinct buyer job and credible practical value beyond a free/trivial substitute. Otherwise set `FAIL` and remove the component from the proposed product; do not add a tab merely because a chapter exists. In particular, `one chapter = one spreadsheet tab` never passes by itself. The architecture report lists only components that pass this gate and briefly states their decision/use, repeat frequency and additional value. If a workbook is a supporting type, the book or primary product must remain useful without irrelevant or decorative tabs.

## Architecture value logic, buyer/WTP validation and anti-trivialization

Keep two gates separate:

- `ARCHITECTURE_VALUE_LOGIC`: an evidence-backed explanation of why a defined buyer could pay, what benefit/mechanism the proposed product offers, what alternatives charge for, and what meaningful value remains beyond a free article, ordinary template, free general AI, consultant or doing nothing. This is a researched market hypothesis, not proof that named buyers will pay.
- `REAL_BUYER_WTP`: validation with actual prospective buyers, such as interviews with behavioral evidence, conversion/paid-test evidence or otherwise suitable direct proof of willingness to pay. It may remain `UNKNOWN` while the owner reviews the architecture.

Before an owner architecture decision, Product/Commercial must pass the architecture value logic in evidence-backed plain language, including a free-AI comparison against the complete job. Strong market and competitor evidence, a grounded buyer/problem hypothesis, coherent product architecture, free-AI distinction, and independent Product/Market/Editorial/Commercial review are required. `REAL_BUYER_WTP = UNKNOWN` does not block showing the complete architecture report. When it is unknown, the report must include: “Это проверенная по рынку архитектурная гипотеза. Готовность конкретных покупателей платить ещё проверяется.” `ARCHITECTURE_VALUE_LOGIC = INCOMPLETE` or `FREE_AI_REPLACEABILITY = HIGH` blocks the architecture handoff: Product/Commercial must RETHINK the paid value, redesign the offer/mechanism, and recheck the evidence. Unknown buyer/WTP evidence does not turn that logic into a confirmed demand claim.

Architecture approval means: “Это направление понятно и достаточно сильно, чтобы проверять его на покупателях / прототипе.” It does not approve a price, prove demand or authorize full production. `REAL_BUYER_WTP = UNKNOWN` blocks `FULL_PRODUCTION` and `RELEASE`; run the buyer-validation step and pass it before those stages, alongside the other applicable Core and domain release gates. If WTP evidence is negative or changes the supported scope, return to Product Factory and revise the architecture.

A calculator, spreadsheet, checklist, PDF, prompt or template can be a useful module, but cannot by itself prove a paid product when the locked job is broader. State its place in the whole product and how it connects to the next user result. Do not let a working file or technically successful build substitute for category, value or product evidence.

## Product Architecture Report before prototype

Before any representative or visual prototype, create the complete `PRODUCT ARCHITECTURE REPORT` as an owner-readable document in simple Russian. This architecture decision package is the one permitted pre-prototype owner gate. It may be shown only after product identity, full-job and discipline research, direct competitor/package depth, paid value, free-AI comparison, knowledge-product depth plan when applicable, portfolio comparison when needed, and independent Product, Market, Editorial and Commercial review have passed. It asks the owner to confirm the understood product direction, not to repair internal research or choose an executor/tool.

The report must cover all eleven parts:

1. Who buys: the person, business/team context and situation.
2. The complete job and specific problem being solved; show `PROMISED_RESULT = TO_DEFINE` if the owner has not approved a promise, then give any recommendation as an unapproved proposal with alternatives/evidence/claims boundary.
3. The discipline map: what a small-business manager genuinely needs to know; relevant methods, models and tools; what applies to small/medium business and what is mainly enterprise-heavy or advanced.
4. Architecture value logic (`ARCHITECTURE_VALUE_LOGIC`): why the offer could be worth paying for, alternatives, evidence and free-AI comparison. Show `REAL_BUYER_WTP` separately; if it is `UNKNOWN`, state that the architecture is a market-tested hypothesis and concrete buyer willingness to pay remains to be checked.
5. Direct comparable self-serve digital products and what is actually in their available previews/samples/package; clearly separate secondary substitutes, price when known, strengths, weaknesses and market lessons.
6. The complete proposed module map. For every module: what it teaches and why; the method; situations where it works/does not; examples/scenarios; practical steps; only workbook/tool items that pass `WORKBOOK_COMPONENT_VALUE`; a filled example when useful; expected learning/use result; and connection to the next module.
7. The buyer journey from purchase/opening through first useful result and final outcome.
8. Product-portfolio recommendation when scope is large: compare one deep master product, a connected series of 2–3 products, and a core plus advanced products against learning depth, usefulness, usability, finishability, buyer motivation, price/value and commercial logic.
9. Format choice, made after the product/module architecture and product-type classification, with a plain-language reason. Show only workbook/tool components that pass their value gate.
10. From Svetlana's source materials and prior product work: what to keep, add, rebuild, exclude/remove and why, plus what is still missing.
11. Material risks and unknowns that may change the product.

For knowledge products, make clear where the buyer receives deep explanation, ready application support, varied real examples, practical scenarios and usable tools, rather than only an assignment to create their own. Do not put unexplained internal shorthand in the report. It must be understandable to a person without product-management, marketing, AI-architecture or Git knowledge. The report must show evidence provenance without becoming a research dump. If any applicable item is unknown or unsupported, repair upstream or keep the architecture decision blocked.

## Two owner decision gates; prototype ordering

Keep the pre-prototype architecture decision distinct from the post-prototype product direction gate:

1. **Architecture decision:** after the gates above and independent Product/Market/Editorial/Commercial review pass, provide the complete report in simple Russian. Confirm the owner understands what is being built, for whom, why it is worth paying for, how it teaches/supports application, its modules, AI distinction, product-series recommendation when relevant and format. Record explicit approval or a specific direction change. Without approval, visual/representative prototype work stays blocked. A scope change returns to Product Factory and relocks the approved identity.
2. **Product/prototype decision:** only after architecture approval, make a meaningful end-to-end slice of the proposed whole system using real content. Show how a buyer reads/uses it, acts, gets a result and moves to the next part. A disconnected micro-tool is not representative. Then close the existing preproduction definition of done below, including artifact QA and independent review; only after all gates pass may Architect package any remaining genuine owner decision about the reviewed artifact/direction.

The architecture approval is a genuine owner decision allowed before a prototype; it does not close or waive the later technical, visual/UX, legal, commercial or independent artifact reviews. A prototype's existence never approves the architecture or closes internal gates.

## Preproduction definition of done (post-architecture prototype gate)

`OWNER_HANDOFF = BLOCKED` until every applicable gate below is closed with evidence:

1. Recovery audit of the current product and canonical sources.
2. Current buyer/problem evidence.
3. Current market, competitor and substitute research.
4. Free-AI replaceability check.
5. Commercial and value logic.
6. Evidence-based format decision.
7. Legal, IP and claims check.
8. Representative prototype made from real product content and rendered, opened or used in its intended medium.
9. Technical QA.
10. Visual and UX QA.
11. Applicable reader-experience gates: adaptive structure, human language across all buyer-facing components, reader flow, instructional depth, distinct section value/cross-module duplication, example realism and whole-product transitions.
12. Tool comprehension for every applicable buyer-facing tool, independently checked by a cold reader.
13. Independent Product Strategy, Market, Instructional Design, Editorial/Reader Experience, Commercial, Legal/IP, UX, Technical and QA Red-Team reviews of the actual prototype/package; cold-reader QA remains independent of the producer.
14. One consolidated repair of correctable review defects.
15. Fresh recheck of repaired criteria and a regression review after repair.

For every gate, record `APPLICABLE / NOT APPLICABLE`, status, source/evidence and date. A skipped gate is valid only as `NOT APPLICABLE` with a specific reason; blank, unknown or incomplete is not closed. If the product is not ready for a representative prototype, continue its missing upstream stages and do not create a premature owner gate.

## Self-heal and routing

For any incomplete or failed but correctable criterion:

`DETECT → ROUTE TO THE RESPONSIBLE SPECIALIST → REPAIR → RECHECK → CONTINUE`.

Route correctable gaps as follows:

| Gap | Responsible specialist |
|---|---|
| Buyer/problem evidence is incomplete | Customer Research / Customer Voice |
| Discipline map or small/medium-business fit is weak | Domain Methodologist + Market Research |
| Market, competitor or substitute evidence is insufficient | Market Research |
| Comparable product contents/packaging are unknown | Market Research (lawful preview/sample inspection) |
| Buyer value or commercial logic is weak | Product/Commercial |
| Knowledge-product teaching depth or practical support is weak | Editorial / Instructional Design |
| Structure is over-templated, reader flow is mechanical, value repeats, examples lack context or transitions jump | Product Strategy + Instructional Design + Editorial/Reader Experience |
| Buyer-facing labels, form fields, warnings or tool explanations are unclear | Editorial/Reader Experience + UX |
| Cold reader cannot explain a tool's inputs, result, decision or limits | UX + Technical + Editorial/Reader Experience |
| Example presents unsourced owner history as fact | Editorial + Legal/IP |
| Product may be too large or too small for one offer | Product/Commercial (master vs series comparison) |
| Text or explanation is weak | Editorial |
| Visual hierarchy or usability is weak | Art/UX |
| Workbook, formula, code or technical behavior is defective | Technical |
| Rights, IP or claims need review | Legal/IP |

Do not ask Светлана whether normal research, review or repair should continue, which specialist/tool/model to use, or to inspect unfinished QA. If a recheck finds another correctable defect, route that defect and continue. If an actual external prerequisite or decision blocks progress, record the exact blocker and the smallest owner action needed.

## Architect pre-handoff block

Before **each** owner handoff, Architect/Supervisor must answer internally: `ARE ALL REQUIRED INTERNAL GATES CLOSED?` The gate list is stage-specific; an architecture decision package and a post-prototype product decision are different handoffs.

- **Architecture decision package:** if any applicable identity, buyer/problem hypothesis, discipline/SMB-fit, strong direct competitor/package evidence, free-AI distinction, `ARCHITECTURE_VALUE_LOGIC`, workbook/tool component value, knowledge-depth, portfolio, format-fit, legal/IP/claims-feasibility or independent Product/Market/Editorial/Commercial review is open, set `ARCHITECTURE_OWNER_HANDOFF = BLOCKED`, assign its specialist and self-heal. `REAL_BUYER_WTP = UNKNOWN` alone does not block this package when the required evidence-backed architecture gates pass; disclose that limit with the exact report wording above. A complete eleven-part report in simple Russian is required. Architect then checks that the owner can understand: what is being built, for whom, whether the promise is approved or `TO_DEFINE`, what the buyer will learn and be able to use, the complete modules and their links, why it could be worth paying for, what free AI does not replace, whether one product or a series is recommended and why, why the format fits, and which precise direction decision is needed. If any answer is **NO**—including whether Svetlana would need another AI/chat to translate—block handoff and repair the report/architecture internally. Owner architecture approval permits validation of the direction, not price approval, proof of demand or full production. - **Product/prototype decision package:** until all applicable gates in the preproduction definition of done below are closed, set `OWNER_HANDOFF = BLOCKED`, list open gates/evidence gaps, assign specialists and route back. `REAL_BUYER_WTP = UNKNOWN` also keeps `FULL_PRODUCTION` and `RELEASE` blocked even when the owner has approved the architecture hypothesis. An architecture approval never authorizes prototype creation before that approval, waives later artifact QA, or means the overall product is complete. A prototype by itself never authorizes owner handoff.

## Owner decision policy

Ask Светлана only for a material choice between valid product directions, informed approval of the complete internally reviewed architecture before prototyping, approval of a fully reviewed representative product/visual direction, permission/access/payment with owner consequences, or a business risk/claim that specialists cannot resolve internally. Do not route ordinary evidence gaps or correctable defects to her. Owner-facing communication starts with meaning, then evidence, then the decision; translate or omit internal terms. The acceptance test is that a person without product-management, marketing, AI-architecture or Git knowledge can understand it on first read without asking another AI/chat to translate it. A simple request such as `Дошей продукт до сильного продаваемого прототипа и покажи мне, когда сам всё проверишь` means Product Factory autonomously restores sources, locks the existing identity, covers the whole discipline/job, researches the actual direct product package, proves practical paid value, chooses an evidence-backed master/series structure when needed, writes and reviews the complete architecture, gets informed pre-prototype approval, builds and reviews a meaningful system slice, repairs and rechecks the artifact, and only then returns a later decision package if a genuine decision remains.

If owner-controlled access, permission, payment or a unique owner-held fact is the exact external prerequisite for closing a required gate, make only a narrowly scoped `EXTERNAL_PREREQUISITE_REQUEST` stating what is needed and which stage it unblocks. This is not an owner acceptance/review handoff: do not present unfinished work for QA or approval, continue all independent stages, and resume the blocked gate when the prerequisite is supplied.

## Evidence budget and anti-loop

At task start, Architect records the question each research pass must resolve, minimum sufficient evidence, source diversity/freshness, time or query budget, diminishing-return stop condition, and reroute threshold. Synthesize when evidence is sufficient; stop repeated searches that add no distinct evidence. Allow at most two materially identical failed repair attempts; on a second repeat, change specialist/method/tool/scope or record `CAPABILITY_GAP` with an exact blocker. Autonomy does not authorize endless research or silent scope reduction.
