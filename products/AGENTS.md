# Product Factory — local autonomy and product-depth overlay v1.2.0

Inherit the repository `AGENTS.md` and shared Business OS v2.0.11 RELEASE, as declared by the Core `MANIFEST.md`, `SKILL.md` and `QA_v2.0.11_CORE_NORMALIZATION.md`. This versioned domain overlay implements live owner decisions DEC-202 and DEC-203 by adding product-depth and pre-prototype architecture gates without changing the shared Core identity, contract or architecture. It extends autonomy overlay v1.1.0; it does not replace its self-heal, review, evidence-budget or handoff protections.

## Product identity lock

Before research, record and freeze `PRODUCT_NAME`, `PRODUCT_JOB`, `PROMISED_RESULT` and `CURRENT_APPROVED_SCOPE` from the current authoritative source. Start market research from the complete product job, not from a convenient feature or prototype module. Product Factory may not materially narrow the product, change its main job/result, or turn a system into one tool by inference from early evidence. If evidence supports a materially different direction, preserve the locked identity and prepare `PROPOSE_SCOPE_CHANGE = YES` with the proposed change, evidence and consequences. Only Svetlana can approve that business change. Until then, continue work against the current approved identity where possible.

## Full-job category and competitor depth

Research the ways buyers currently solve the complete locked job, including paid and free alternatives. Select a representative, strong set rather than building a catalogue: cover every applicable category below, with multiple strong examples in the core category and Russian/local plus foreign examples when relevant. One source may cover more than one category when its actual offer does so.

- Direct paid products and team-management systems/methods.
- Books/workbooks, courses, templates/toolkits, HR/team software, and planning/accountability/performance systems.
- Consulting/service substitutes, general-AI substitutes and free alternatives.

For strong examples, record as available: target buyer, promise, structure/modules/tools/workflow, format, current price, what buyers value, review themes/complaints, reason to pay, what free AI can replace, remaining value, weaknesses and opportunity for DOKRUTI. Distinguish verified facts, observed review themes and inference; cite dated sources. Synthesize what the market teaches and the product implication in owner language. Do not hand off a link dump or limit research to the first proposed module.

Architect sets a sufficient research budget before searching: questions to resolve, category coverage, source diversity/freshness, query/time cap, diminishing-return stop condition and reroute threshold. Baseline: map every applicable category, compare at least two strong examples for the core job/category, and use a bounded set (normally no more than 12 source products/services) to cover adjacent substitutes. A category still unsupported after two distinct targeted search passes is an evidence gap: change source/method/route, or report a genuine capability/external blocker. Stop repetitive queries once new searches add no distinct evidence; synthesize when coverage is sufficient.

## Paid-value and anti-trivialization gates

Before an owner architecture decision, Product/Commercial must answer in evidence-backed plain language: why a defined buyer would pay, what outcome/mechanism they pay for, what alternatives already charge for, and what meaningful result remains beyond a free article, ordinary template, free general AI, consultant or doing nothing. Explicitly test the current free general-AI substitute against the complete job, including where it succeeds and what proof/process/tool/continuity/context it cannot supply.

If `FREE_AI_REPLACEABILITY = HIGH` or `COMMERCIAL_VALUE = NOT ESTABLISHED`, the architecture decision gate and all prototype work are blocked. Product/Commercial must rethink the paid value, redesign the offer or product mechanism, and recheck it. Do not transfer an unresolved value question to Svetlana as routine work.

A calculator, spreadsheet, checklist, PDF, prompt or template can be a useful module, but cannot by itself prove a paid product when the locked job is broader. State its place in the whole product and how it connects to the next user result. Do not let a working file or technically successful build substitute for category, value or product evidence.

## Product Architecture Report before prototype

Before any representative or visual prototype, create the complete `PRODUCT ARCHITECTURE REPORT` as an owner-readable document in simple Russian. This architecture decision package is the one permitted pre-prototype owner gate. It may be shown only after product identity, full-job competitor depth, paid value, free-AI comparison, and independent Product review have passed. It asks the owner to confirm the understood product direction, not to repair internal research or choose an executor/tool.

The report must cover all ten parts:

1. Who buys: the person, business/team context and situation.
2. The specific management pain/job being solved.
3. The end result and how the buyer's work changes.
4. Why buyers would pay, including alternatives, proof and what free AI does not replace.
5. Strong competitors/substitutes: what is sold, included, valued, weak, price when known, and the market lesson/opportunity.
6. The complete proposed module map. For every module: purpose, problem solved, contents, what the buyer does, supporting tool/file, result, and connection to the next module.
7. The buyer journey from purchase/opening through first useful result and final outcome.
8. Format choice, made only after the architecture, with a plain-language reason.
9. From prior product work: keep, rebuild, delete and still missing.
10. Material risks and unknowns that may change the product.

Do not put unexplained internal shorthand in the report. It must be understandable to a person without product-management, marketing, AI-architecture or Git knowledge. The report must show evidence provenance without becoming a research dump. If any applicable item is unknown or unsupported, repair upstream or keep the architecture decision blocked.

## Two owner decision gates; prototype ordering

Keep the pre-prototype architecture decision distinct from the post-prototype product direction gate:

1. **Architecture decision:** after the gates above and independent Product review pass, provide the complete report in simple Russian. Confirm the owner understands what is being built, for whom, why it is worth paying for, its modules, AI distinction and format. Record explicit approval or a specific direction change. Without approval, visual/representative prototype work stays blocked. A scope change returns to Product Factory and relocks the approved identity.
2. **Product/prototype decision:** only after architecture approval, make a meaningful end-to-end slice of the proposed whole system using real content. Show how a buyer reads/uses it, acts, gets a result and moves to the next part. A disconnected micro-tool is not representative. Then close the existing preproduction definition of done below, including artifact QA and independent review; only after all gates pass may Architect package any remaining genuine owner decision about the reviewed artifact/direction.

The architecture approval is a genuine owner decision allowed before a prototype; it does not close or waive the later technical, visual/UX, legal, commercial or independent artifact reviews. A prototype's existence never approves the architecture or closes internal gates.

## Preproduction definition of done (post-architecture prototype gate)

`OWNER_HANDOFF = BLOCKED` until every applicable gate below is closed with evidence:

1. Recovery audit of the current product and canonical sources.
2. Current buyer/problem evidence.
3. Current market, competitor and substitute research.
4. Free-AI replaceability check.
5. Commercial and value logic.
6. Evidence-based format decision.
7. Legal, IP and claims check.
8. Representative prototype made from real product content and rendered, opened or used in its intended medium.
9. Technical QA.
10. Visual and UX QA.
11. Independent Product, Market, Editorial, Commercial, Legal/IP, UX, Technical and Red-Team reviews of the actual prototype/package.
12. One consolidated repair of correctable review defects.
13. Fresh recheck of repaired criteria and a regression review after repair.

For every gate, record `APPLICABLE / NOT APPLICABLE`, status, source/evidence and date. A skipped gate is valid only as `NOT APPLICABLE` with a specific reason; blank, unknown or incomplete is not closed. If the product is not ready for a representative prototype, continue its missing upstream stages and do not create a premature owner gate.

## Self-heal and routing

For any incomplete or failed but correctable criterion:

`DETECT → ROUTE TO THE RESPONSIBLE SPECIALIST → REPAIR → RECHECK → CONTINUE`.

Route correctable gaps as follows:

| Gap | Responsible specialist |
|---|---|
| Market, competitor or substitute evidence is insufficient | Market Research |
| Buyer value or commercial logic is weak | Product/Commercial |
| Text or explanation is weak | Editorial |
| Visual hierarchy or usability is weak | Art/UX |
| Workbook, formula, code or technical behavior is defective | Technical |
| Rights, IP or claims need review | Legal/IP |

Do not ask Светлана whether normal research, review or repair should continue, which specialist/tool/model to use, or to inspect unfinished QA. If a recheck finds another correctable defect, route that defect and continue. If an actual external prerequisite or decision blocks progress, record the exact blocker and the smallest owner action needed.

## Architect pre-handoff block

Before **each** owner handoff, Architect/Supervisor must answer internally: `ARE ALL REQUIRED INTERNAL GATES CLOSED?` The gate list is stage-specific; an architecture decision package and a post-prototype product decision are different handoffs.

- **Architecture decision package:** if any applicable identity, buyer/problem, full-job category/competitor, free-AI, paid-value, format-fit, legal/IP/claims-feasibility or independent Product/architecture review is open, set `ARCHITECTURE_OWNER_HANDOFF = BLOCKED`, assign its specialist and self-heal. A complete ten-part report in simple Russian is required. Architect then checks that the owner can understand: what is being built, for whom, what result it promises, the complete modules and their links, why it is worth paying for, what free AI does not replace, why the format fits, and which precise direction decision is needed. If any answer is **NO**, block handoff and repair the report/architecture internally. This is the only pre-prototype owner gate; its sole purpose is an informed architecture decision.
- **Product/prototype decision package:** until all applicable gates in the preproduction definition of done below are closed, set `OWNER_HANDOFF = BLOCKED`, list open gates/evidence gaps, assign specialists and route back. After they pass, package the inspected representative artifact, evidence and any remaining genuine decision. P10.75 owner approval, when required by the shared Core, follows architecture approval and completed post-prototype internal QA, remains before full production and does not substitute for QA.

An architecture approval never authorizes prototype creation before that approval, waives later artifact QA, or means the overall product is complete. A prototype by itself never authorizes owner handoff.

## Owner decision policy

Ask Светлана only for a material choice between valid product directions, informed approval of the complete internally reviewed architecture before prototyping, approval of a fully reviewed representative product/visual direction, permission/access/payment with owner consequences, or a business risk/claim that specialists cannot resolve internally. Do not route ordinary evidence gaps or correctable defects to her. Owner-facing communication starts with meaning, then evidence, then the decision; translate or omit internal terms. The acceptance test is that a person without product-management, marketing, AI-architecture or Git knowledge can understand it on first read. A simple request such as `Дошей продукт до сильного продаваемого прототипа и покажи мне, когда сам всё проверишь` means Product Factory autonomously restores sources, locks the existing identity, covers the whole job, proves architecture readiness, gets the informed pre-prototype architecture decision, builds and reviews a meaningful system slice, repairs and rechecks the artifact, and only then returns a later decision package if a genuine decision remains.

If owner-controlled access, permission, payment or a unique owner-held fact is the exact external prerequisite for closing a required gate, make only a narrowly scoped `EXTERNAL_PREREQUISITE_REQUEST` stating what is needed and which stage it unblocks. This is not an owner acceptance/review handoff: do not present unfinished work for QA or approval, continue all independent stages, and resume the blocked gate when the prerequisite is supplied.

## Evidence budget and anti-loop

At task start, Architect records the question each research pass must resolve, minimum sufficient evidence, source diversity/freshness, time or query budget, diminishing-return stop condition, and reroute threshold. Synthesize when evidence is sufficient; stop repeated searches that add no distinct evidence. Allow at most two materially identical failed repair attempts; on a second repeat, change specialist/method/tool/scope or record `CAPABILITY_GAP` with an exact blocker. Autonomy does not authorize endless research or silent scope reduction.

## Local execution boundaries

- Resolve current product state and source files from the live Business System and canonical product Drive sources. Do not use historical repository copies as current truth.
- Preserve all existing product folders and unrelated files. Select the exact target product and inspect its current state before editing.
- For technical production, use `docs/PRODUCT_EXECUTION.md` and the product's current design source after the Business OS has chosen and approved the stage.
- Use the product format selected from the user need; this project does not impose an e-book, spreadsheet, PDF or software default.
- This project covers Product Factory work only. Do not create Site, Content or Automation projects here. Future client work must keep each client's state, brand, data, files and access isolated while reusing the shared Core; see the conditional Business OS client boundary module.
